Nivel A1: Nivel básico

1. Greetings & Basics

  • Good morning (Buenos días)
  • Good night (Buenas noches)
  • Nice to meet you (Encantado)
  • How are you? (¿Cómo estás?)
  • Excuse me (Disculpe)
  • Please / Thanks (Por favor / Gracias)
  • You're welcome (De nada)
  • See you later (Hasta luego)
  • Welcome (Bienvenido)
  • I am sorry (Lo siento)

Grammar: The Verb To Be

Describe quiénes somos, nacionalidades y estados.

  • Affirmative: I am, You are, He/She/It is.
  • Negative: I am not, You aren't, He isn't.
  • Question: Are you...? / Is he...?

2. Family & House

  • Mother / Father (Madre / Padre)
  • Brother / Sister (Hermano /a)
  • Uncle / Aunt (Tío /a)
  • Kitchen (Cocina)
  • Living room (Salón)
  • Son / Daughter (Hijo/a)
  • Grandparents (Abuelos)
  • Door / Window (Puerta / Ventana)
  • Bedroom (Habitación)
  • Bathroom (Baño)

Grammar: Possessives

Indican a quién pertenece un objeto o relación.

  • My / Your: My name / Your house.
  • His / Her: His car / Her dog.
  • Our / Their: Our class / Their job.

3. Numbers & Time

  • Numbers: 1, 20, 30, 50, 100
  • Days: Monday to Sunday
  • Months: January to December
  • Seasons: Spring, Summer...
  • Clock: O'clock, Half past
  • Period: Morning, Noon, Night
  • Relative: Today, Tomorrow
  • Date: What's the date?

Grammar: Prepositions

Palabras clave para situar tiempo y fechas.

  • AT : Horas y noche. (at 8:00)
  • ON : Días y fechas específicas. (On monday.)
  • IN : Meses, años y mañanas. (In two months.)

4. Daily Verbs

  • Eat / Drink (Comer / Beber)
  • Work / Study (Trabajar / Estudiar)
  • Read / Write (Leer / Escribir)
  • Listen / Speak (Escuchar / Hablar)
  • Wake up (Despertarse)
  • Watch TV (Ver tele)
  • Go to bed (Ir a dormir)
  • Cook (Cocinar)
  • Play sports (Hacer deporte)
  • Clean up (Limpiar)

Grammar: Present Simple

Se usa para rutinas diarias y hechos generales.

  • Affirmative: I play / He plays (-s).
  • Negative: I don't / She doesn't work.
  • Question: Do you...? / Does he...?
